# Begin with ORYNTH

ORYNTH is constitutional execution-governance and runtime-integrity infrastructure.

Its central distinction is simple:

> Capability does not establish authority.

A system may be technically capable of performing an action without possessing the authority, admissibility, continuity, or evidentiary basis required to execute it.
