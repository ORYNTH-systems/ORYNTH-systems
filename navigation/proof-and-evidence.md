# Proof and Evidence

ORYNTH treats evidence as part of execution integrity—not as an explanation added after an action has already occurred.

The proof surface addresses:

- authority binding;
- admissibility;
- constraint satisfaction;
- execution-state evidence;
- continuity;
- auditability;
- verification;
- consequential boundaries.
